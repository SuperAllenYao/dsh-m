# v2.16.0

## 中文

- 改进：
  - 超长会话滚动整体变流畅：几千条消息的会话也能连续快速滑动，打开会话或翻看历史不再卡顿。
  - 历史加载中快速滚动不掉帧：往回翻历史时，顶部进度条还在走也能随意快速滑动，几乎感觉不到掉帧（此前长消息较多的会话在此期间会明显卡一下）。
  - 超长消息一次成型：很长的回复整条直接呈现，滑过时不再一顿一顿地出现。
  - 巨型过程组（上百个步骤）展开、收起同样顺滑：长内容会在一瞬间内分批补齐，不再出现卡死、组头被截断或展开后的大片空白。
- 修复：
  - 超大过程组里点「展开其余 N 步」不再失灵，点一下就能看到全部步骤。
  - 快速滚动回到历史区域时，列表不再出现内容高度反复跳变。

## English

- Improvements:
  - Scrolling in very long sessions is smooth overall: even sessions with thousands of messages can be swiped through quickly, and opening a session or browsing back through history no longer stutters.
  - Fast scrolling during history loading no longer drops frames: while the top progress bar is still running, you can swipe freely and quickly with barely any noticeable jank (previously, sessions with many long messages visibly stuttered during this phase).
  - Long messages render in one piece: a very long reply appears whole as you scroll past it, instead of showing up in halting chunks.
  - Giant process groups (a hundred-plus steps) expand and collapse smoothly too: long content fills in within a blink in small batches, with no freeze, no clipped header, and no large blank area after expanding.
- Fixes:
  - Tapping "show the remaining N steps" inside a very large process group works again — one tap reveals every step.
  - When scrolling quickly back into the history area, the list no longer jumps back and forth in content height.
