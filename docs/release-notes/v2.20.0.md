# v2.20.0

## 中文

- 改进：
  - 会话详情底部的「子代理 / 后台任务」入口更紧凑：图标与数字两侧的留白减半，不再显得空。
  - 子代理列表与后台任务列表的顶部标签，点整块区域都能切换，不用只点文字。
  - 归档确认弹窗的两个按钮，点按钮区域内任何位置都能生效，不用只点文字。

- 修复：
  - 置顶、取消置顶不再有飞入/淡入动画：点一下就立即在置顶区出现、或立即回到原工作区，列表不会闪烁弹动。
  - 会话卡片之间、置顶卡片之间、置顶卡片上方的灰色横线已去掉，列表恢复干净的卡片间距。

## English

- Improvements:
  - The shared "subagents / background tasks" entry at the bottom of the session detail is now more compact: the side padding around the icon and count is halved.
  - In the subagent and background-task lists, the whole tab area is tappable, not just the label text.
  - Both buttons in the archive confirmation dialog respond anywhere inside their area, not only on the text.

- Fixes:
  - Pinning and unpinning no longer animate: the session appears in the pinned area immediately, or returns to its workspace immediately, without the list flashing or bouncing.
  - The grey horizontal lines between session cards, between pinned cards, and above the pinned cards are gone.
