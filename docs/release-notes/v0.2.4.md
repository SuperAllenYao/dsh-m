# v0.2.4

## 中文

- 新增：
  - Mac 更换网络后地址自动跟随更新，不再把失效的旧地址发给手机。
  - 打开会话时若连接未就绪先自动排队，就绪后立即打开；超过 10 秒才提示可重试，不再无限转圈。
- 改进：
  - 会话页流量大幅下降：后台刷新的数据量减至原来的约六分之一，蜂窝与中继网络下感受最明显。
- 修复：
  - 修复移动网络抖动时反复出现「连接无响应，正在重连…」、重连风暴长达 25 秒的问题。
  - 修复首次安装后冷启动打开会话要等十几秒甚至 28 秒的问题（现在约 1 秒）。
  - 修复点进会话先看 3–4 秒转圈才出内容的问题（现在 1 秒内出内容）。
  - 修复个别地址校验失败就误判「需要重新配对」的问题。

## English

- New:
  - After the Mac changes networks, its address updates itself instead of handing the phone a dead one.
  - Opening a session before the link is ready now queues it and opens it the moment the link is up; only after 10 seconds do you get a retry prompt instead of an infinite spinner.
- Improvements:
  - Session pages use far less data: background refresh traffic drops to about a sixth, most noticeable on cellular and relay.
- Fixes:
  - Fixed the "no response, reconnecting…" banner and ~25-second reconnect storms under cellular jitter.
  - Fixed first-install cold starts taking 10-plus (up to 28) seconds to open a session — now about 1 second.
  - Fixed a 3–4 second spinner before session content; content now shows within a second.
  - Fixed being wrongly told to re-pair when a single address failed its check.
