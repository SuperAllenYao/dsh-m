# v2.22.1

## 中文

- 修复：
  - 置顶或取消置顶会话时，列表会整屏跳一下、卡片闪一下的问题已修复；现在卡片沿同一条曲线连续让位，整个过程流畅不闪。
  - 在列表最底部置顶会话时不再突然跳动；列表会平滑下移一小段，底部不留空白。
  - 取消置顶后，卡片回到所在分组的位置，不再反向飞出或突然消失；左滑按钮也不会误露出。
  - 动画播完后列表偶尔会快速闪一下、连续置顶/取消置顶时卡片偶尔又跳回置顶区的问题已修复；现在动画结束的一刻列表就直接停在最终位置。

- 改进：
  - 连续快速置顶或取消、或在动画播放中收到新的会话变化时，动画会立即让位给最新操作并接着播完，不再卡顿或回跳；动画结束后列表保持稳定，不会持续刷新。

## English

- Fixes:
  - Pinning or unpinning a session no longer makes the whole list jump or the cards flicker. Cards now move along one continuous curve, so the transition stays smooth.
  - Pinning a session while at the very bottom of the list no longer jumps; the list glides down a little and leaves no blank space at the bottom.
  - After unpinning, the card returns to its place in its group instead of flying back or disappearing abruptly, and swipe actions no longer flash open by mistake.
  - The list could flash for an instant right after the animation, and a card could briefly jump back into the pinned area during repeated pin/unpin. Both are fixed: the list now stays exactly where the animation leaves it.

- Improvements:
  - Fast repeated pin/unpin, or a list update arriving mid-animation, now yields to your latest action and finishes the new transition without stutter or bounce. Once the animation settles, the list stays still and stops refreshing.
