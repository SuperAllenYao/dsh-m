# v2.10.0

## 中文

- 新增：
  - 消息列表右缘新增滚动指示条：滑块贴底代表最新、贴顶代表最旧，滚动时浮现、停稳后淡出。
- 改进：
  - 消息列表彻底重排：最新消息天然贴底，后台补历史在视觉上零位移——从原理上杜绝内容跳动。
  - 翻历史时新消息绝不推走正在读的内容（与 iMessage 行为一致），回到底部恢复跟随。
- 修复：
  - 修复无缓存首次进入长会话时、补历史期间画面反复微跳的问题。
  - 修复滚动条首版位置与显示的两处缺陷。

## English

- New:
  - A scroll indicator on the right edge of the message list: the thumb at the bottom means newest, at the top means oldest; it fades in while scrolling and out when settled.
- Improvements:
  - The message list was rebuilt so the newest message sits at the bottom by construction — background history fills with zero visible shift, eliminating content jumps at the root.
  - While reading history, new messages never push what you're reading (matching iMessage); following resumes when you return to the bottom.
- Fixes:
  - Fixed residual micro-jitter while history filled in on a first, cache-less visit to a long session.
  - Fixed two first-version flaws of the indicator (position and display).
