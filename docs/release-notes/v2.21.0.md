# v2.21.0

## 中文

- 新增：
  - 会话列表支持左滑：向左滑动卡片即露出「置顶 / 归档」两个操作，松手后停在展开位置；反向右滑、点卡片以外区域或点卡片本身都能收起；快速整滑不会误触操作。

- 改进：
  - 点「置顶」时卡片带一点回弹飞入置顶区、并在途中变成紧凑卡；取消置顶立即归位。整个过程不再整屏闪烁。
  - 列表卡片状态更及时：等待作答、等待审批、进行中、空闲随会话实时变化，多处等待时显示数量。
  - 运行中的会话（含子代理会话）上下滑动更跟手，长时间滚动不再掉帧。
  - Mac 锁屏或 App 退到后台后回到前台，约 1 秒内自动恢复连接，不用手动重连。

- 修复：
  - 左滑展开后按钮点不到、松手就弹回、右滑收不起来的问题已修好。
  - 新建会话的详情页现在会显示桌面端默认使用的模型；还没有用量时不显示上下文百分比，与桌面端保持一致。
  - 打开正在运行的子代理会话时不再崩溃，进入后滚动也稳定。

## English

- New:
  - Sessions can be swiped left to reveal "Pin / Archive". The card stays open after you let go; swipe back to the right, tap elsewhere, or tap the card itself to close it. A fast full swipe never triggers an action by accident.

- Improvements:
  - Pinning now flies the card into the pinned area with a light bounce and morphs it into the compact card on the way; unpinning puts it back right away. The list no longer flashes.
  - Card statuses stay current: waiting for your answer, waiting for approval, running and idle update live, with counts when several sessions wait.
  - Scrolling running sessions (including subagent sessions) feels smoother and no longer drops frames during long runs.
  - After your Mac's screen locks or the app goes to the background, coming back reconnects automatically in about a second — no manual reconnect.

- Fixes:
  - Swipe actions that could not be tapped, cards that bounced back on release, and a right swipe that would not close the card are all fixed.
  - A new session's detail page now shows the model your desktop uses by default; context usage stays hidden until there is real usage, matching the desktop.
  - Opening a running subagent session no longer crashes, and scrolling inside it stays stable.
