# v2.23.0

## 中文

- 新增：
  - 一键导出网络日志，两端都有：手机在设置页「网络日志」段点一下即可导出并直接分享；Mac 在「DSH-m 手机连接 › 本地数据」卡片点「导出网络日志」即可下载。
  - 连接过程自动记录，不需要打开任何开关：切网与重连的时间、当时所处的网络环境、尝试了哪些地址、每个候选的结果、时间花在哪一段，都能在导出包内查到。手机保留最近 72 小时并限制在 20MB 内，Mac 导出插件原有的完整审计日志。
  - 遇到异常自动留档：重连超过 2 秒或一轮里多个地址都失败时，自动保留事件前后各 60 秒的日志快照，独立保存 7 天，导出包内单独成目录。
  - 两端日志可对账：同一次连接在手机与 Mac 使用同一个标识，跨端排查时可直接对上。
  - 导出包内附机器可读的连接元数据与人可读摘要，方便自己看或转发给他人排查。

- 修复：
  - 桌面端偶发整体退出：Mac 端在推送服务连接超时的情况下没有正确处理异常，会导致 DSH 桌面端直接关闭；现在会安静地结束这次推送并在后续重试，不再影响桌面端使用。

## English

- New:
  - One-tap network-log export on both sides: a row in the iPhone Settings exports and shares immediately; a button in the Mac "DSH-m Phone Connection › Local data" card downloads the archive.
  - Connection activity is recorded automatically, with nothing to switch on: when the network changed and what it looked like, which addresses were tried, how each candidate turned out and where time went are all in the export. The phone keeps the last 72 hours within 20 MB; the Mac exports the complete existing audit log.
  - Trouble is captured automatically: when a reconnect takes longer than 2 seconds or several addresses fail in one round, a log snapshot covering the 60 seconds before and after is kept separately for 7 days, in its own folder inside the export.
  - The two logs line up: one connection carries the same identifier on the phone and the Mac, so cross-device troubleshooting is direct.
  - The archive includes machine-readable connection metadata and a human-readable summary, ready to review yourself or hand to someone else.

- Fixes:
  - The desktop app could quit unexpectedly: the Mac side did not handle a failed push-service connection properly, which closed DSH entirely; it now ends that attempt quietly and retries later, without affecting normal use.
