# v2.9.0

## 中文

- 新增：
  - 再次打开会话秒进：最近 300 条消息本地缓存，直接完整呈现并停在最新位置。
  - 会话页顶部新增一条纤细的进度条，后台补齐更早历史时安静可见、不打扰。
  - 工作区列表视觉收尾：版本与统计内联、两行名称、更精致的展开动效。
- 改进：
  - 长会话更轻快：只渲染最近 300 行并跟随新内容；往回翻时每次平滑扩展 100 行。
  - 滚动更稳更安静：翻阅时内容不再跳动，静置时列表绝不自行滚动。
- 修复：
  - 修复进入会话后第一次滑动整屏跳变的问题。
  - 修复静置或切后台回来后列表自行滚动、末行被裁或底部出空档的问题。
  - 修复退出重进后会话位置错位（相差数千像素）的问题。

## English

- New:
  - Sessions reopen instantly: the newest 300 messages are cached locally, shown complete and positioned at the latest.
  - A slim progress bar at the top of the session page quietly shows older history filling in.
  - Workspace list polish: inline version and stats, two-line names, refined expand animations.
- Improvements:
  - Long sessions feel lighter: only the newest 300 rows render and follow new content; scrolling back smoothly extends 100 rows at a time.
  - Smoother, calmer scrolling: content no longer jumps while browsing, and the list never scrolls itself while idle.
- Fixes:
  - Fixed the full-screen jump on the first swipe after opening a session.
  - Fixed the list scrolling by itself while idle or after returning from the background, clipped last rows and bottom gaps.
  - Fixed the position being off by thousands of pixels after leaving and re-entering a session.
